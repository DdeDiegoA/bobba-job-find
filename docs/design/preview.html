Boba Job Find — a warm, playful, local-first desktop job-search app. Soft rounded shapes, a tea/tapioca palette, and a calm, honest coach against job-search stress. Light + dark, EN + ES. Toggle the theme (top right) to see the semantic layer swap.

Voice

Warm, honest, encouraging coach.
“We do the steeping. You pick the pearls.”

Two axes, always

Fit — can you get it? · Quality — do you want it?
Never blended into one opaque number.

Never color alone

Every Verdict = number + band word + shape glyph. Grayscale-safe.

Verdict bands — color + number + word + glyph
