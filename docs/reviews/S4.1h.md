# Review — S4.1h HttpClient hardening (reviewer-3)

- Branch: `hermad/S4.1h`, reviewed head `24db6e1` (dev-3), based on main `0e3cf9e`.
- Scope: `src-tauri/crates/boba-sources/src/http/mod.rs` only.
- Verdict: **APPROVED** (after one round of fixes).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC1 — one bucket per origin, strictest interval.** `acquire_slot` keys on
  origin only. The interval is the max of the manifest `min_interval_ms`, the
  global default and 1 ms. A stricter interval replaces the limiter and carries
  over the time since the last start. Tests:
  `same_host_sources_share_one_bucket_at_the_strictest_interval`,
  `a_laxer_source_keeps_the_strictest_host_interval`. Pass.
- **AC2 — cross-origin redirect not followed silently.** The shared client uses
  `Policy::none()`. The 3xx and its `Location` reach the adapter through
  `HttpResponse.location`. Test:
  `cross_origin_redirect_to_a_robots_disallowed_path_is_not_followed` (wiremock
  canned response, the module's house pattern). Pass.

## Round 1 — blocking, fixed in `24db6e1`

- **Robots bypass (AD-10, fail-open).** `Policy::none()` also covered the
  robots.txt fetch, and any non-200 meant allow-all. A robots.txt that redirects
  (http→https, apex→www) was silently ignored.
- **Fix verified in code:** `fetch_robots` follows robots.txt redirects by hand,
  up to 5 hops (6 requests total), with `acquire_slot` per hop. A 3xx with no
  usable Location, a non-http(s) target, or hop overflow returns a synthetic
  disallow-all. 404 or unreachable stays allow-all, as before.
- **Tests:** `redirected_robots_is_followed_and_still_blocks`,
  `robots_redirect_without_a_location_fails_closed`,
  `robots_redirect_loop_fails_closed`. Pass.

## Non-blocking notes

- Requests already waiting on a replaced limiter can briefly bypass a tightened
  interval. Dev-3 documented this as a shortcut in code.
- A robots.txt 5xx still resolves to allow-all after retries. This predates
  S4.1h. RFC 9309 treats 5xx as a full disallow. Suggest a follow-up story.

  `tests/fixtures/http/`. Accepted as the module's house pattern.
