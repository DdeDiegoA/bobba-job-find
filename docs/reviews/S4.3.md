## Findings

### F1 — generic canonical URL merges different jobs (medium, CONFIRMED by reading)
`boba-domain/src/dedup/mod.rs:73-74`. The `CanonicalUrl` check runs first and returns before the location guard (`same_place`, line 82) and before any title check. A canonical URL with no job id (a company careers index, a board home page) is therefore decisive.
- Repro: Offer A `"Backend Engineer"`, Remote, `apply.url = https://acme.com/careers`. Offer B `"Data Analyst"`, Madrid, `apply.url = https://acme.com/careers/`. `duplicate_of(B, A)` → `Some(CanonicalUrl)`; `ingest_deduped` merges B into A.
- Expected: two postings (different title, different place).
- Actual: one posting, B's provenance attached to A.
- Fix direction: only treat a canonical URL as decisive when it carries a job-specific path or query (not a bare host / index path), or let a contradicting `job_key` or location veto it. Add a unit test for the generic-careers-URL case.

### F2 — same-company description merge ignores the title (medium, PLAUSIBLE, not yet reproduced)
`boba-domain/src/dedup/mod.rs:82-94`. Once `same_place` passes, a simhash ≥ 0.9 merges with no title comparison. Two different roles at one company in one location that share a long "About us" boilerplate and differ only in a short role paragraph could reach ≥ 0.9 and merge. The golden set does not cover this: its same-company pairs have different descriptions, and its identical-description pairs are split by city.
- Repro to try: two distinct roles, same company, both Remote, ~150 words each of shared boilerplate plus a short distinct role paragraph; measure `similarity(...)`.
- Expected: not merged unless titles are compatible (or a stricter threshold applies when the titles differ).
- Fix direction: add a golden case for it; if it reproduces, require title agreement (or a fuzzy title match) for the `Description` reason.

### F3 — dedup is not wired into production ingest (medium, CONFIRMED by grep)
The only production ingest path, `boba-app/src/ingest/manual/mod.rs:152`, still calls `normalize::ingest`. Nothing outside `ingest/dedup/` calls `ingest_deduped`. AC1 ("when they are ingested, then they merge") therefore does not hold in the running app: two manual intakes of the same job still create two postings.
- Repro: `ingest_manual_url` twice with the same job on two URLs (same company, title, location). Expected: one posting, two provenance rows. Actual: two postings.
- Fix: route manual intake through `ingest_deduped` (one line plus its `Outcome` handling), with a test on the manual path. If Diego and the orquestador decide the daily-search wiring (S5.2) owns this, record that explicitly in the story and the review, and the AC is partly deferred.
