# Studio

The Studio is where a saved Offer becomes a tailored CV and cover letter that still sounds like
you.

## Start

Open an application from the **Tracker** (or an Offer) and have your agent CLI draft the CV and
cover letter. The drafts land in the Studio with a reviewer's findings. The language matches the
posting: English or Spanish. For a posting in another language, Boba drafts in English and says so.

## Facts, not fiction

Every line Boba writes is tied to facts from your **Profile**. In the Studio you can see which
facts back each line. A line that names something your Profile doesn't have is flagged ("Not in
your Profile"), and a draft with a flagged line isn't marked verified. Fix the line or add the
fact to your Profile.

Documents are written in *your* professional voice, not Boba's. The agent's text is shown as
information to read, never as instructions.

## Editing

- **Edit** any line and **Save**.
- **Regenerate** one section without touching the rest.
- **Show diff** to see what changed since an earlier version. Each change creates a new
  version.
- If the CV is over the page budget, Boba cuts the least relevant lines first and shows you
  exactly what it removed.

## Checks

Render the draft to run three checks:

1. **Facts.** Every line stands on a Profile fact.
2. **Layout.** Pages within budget, no heading stranded at the bottom of a page.
3. **ATS.** The text layer carries every detail, and shows keyword coverage against the posting.

When all three pass, the document is **verified**. Only verified documents are attached when
Boba sends an application.

## Export

Save an ATS-friendly **PDF** or a **DOCX** and use them anywhere. You can export a draft that
isn't verified yet, with a warning, but sending is gated on verification.

## Screening answers

For forms that ask extra questions, Boba drafts answers from your Profile. Nothing is used until
you **approve** it. Approved answers go into your answer bank for next time.
