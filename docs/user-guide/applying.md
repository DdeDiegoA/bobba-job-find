# Applying

You choose how much Boba does for you: you review every application, or Boba sends the ones
that clear rules you wrote. Either way, you can stop it instantly.

## Channels

For each Offer, Boba works out how it can be applied to:

- **Manual.** Boba opens the original page and gives you an **Apply kit**: your verified PDFs,
  your approved answers, one-click copy. Press **Mark applied** when you've sent it.
- **Email Apply.** Boba prepares an email from your connected mailbox with your documents
  attached. In Review mode you read it and press **Send application**.
- **ATS form.** With form filling turned on, Boba opens your own Chrome on a separate profile and
  fills the form while you watch. It **never presses submit**, never solves a CAPTCHA and never
  signs in for you. You check the page and submit it yourself, then press **I submitted it**.
  If Boba isn't sure about a field, it stops and leaves that field to you.
- **Session board.** Only through your own session, with the opt-in described in
  [Sources and connections](sources-and-connections.md).

An application only sends with documents that have passed the Studio checks. Before it goes,
Boba also checks for a **duplicate application** to the same company and warns you.

## Review mode (the default)

Boba prepares, you decide. Every send is yours.

## Auto mode

**Settings → Apply**. Auto stays off until you add at least one **Auto Rule** and confirm that
you understand it.

A rule says:

- which Sources and channels it covers,
- how good an Offer must be (minimum Rank, Fit, Confidence, Verdict),
- whether flagged Offers are allowed,
- the daily cap, the per-company cap, and the active hours.

Defaults: **5 a day** per rule, **10 a day** across all rules (you can raise the global cap, up to
25), and **1 application per company every 30 days**. Automatic sends are spaced **5-20
minutes** apart, inside your active hours. Offers with a fraud badge are never sent
automatically.

Want to see Boba's thinking? **Why would Auto send this?** lets you pick an application and see
the decision, with reasons, before anything happens.

### Dry run

A new rule starts as a **dry run**: it only reports what it *would* have sent. Review the
reports, then **Activate rule** when you're happy.

### The Outbox

An automatic send doesn't leave immediately. It waits in the **Outbox** (5 minutes to 24 hours,
your choice; session boards always at least 60). While it waits you can **Edit** the message or
**Cancel** it, and Boba notifies you that something is queued. If anything looks off, Boba holds
the send for you instead of guessing.

### The Kill Switch

One press stops every automatic send: in the app, or from the **tray menu**. Held sends move to
"Needs you" so nothing is lost, and nothing leaves until you turn the switch off. It is always
a safe thing to press.

### Caps and the audit log

The core, not the AI, enforces every cap and rule. Every automatic send is written to the
**Audit Log** (Settings → Privacy), along with the reasons for every refusal, and the funnel
stats show what's working.

## Your Tracker

Applications move across **Saved, Preparing, Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Hired**, plus
**Rejected, No Response, Withdrawn**. Drag cards or use the keyboard, and undo a move. Each
card keeps a snapshot of the Offer, the package you sent and a timeline. Boba nudges you about
follow-ups, calmly. If you've connected reading, replies show up as **Status Proposals** that
you accept or ignore.
